# Vision | Mythfits

## Why we exist
Every idea starts as an ember. Someone notices a problem, imagines a better way, or wishes something existed. Too many worthwhile ideas never reach the world because their originators lack the technical skills, resources or connections to develop them.

Mythfits exists to help open that door. Our aim is to bring engineering, open minds and experimentation to ideas that help people live more fully, strengthen communities and care for the planet. Technology should improve the human experience, not replace it.

## What we do today
Start with a human-centered engineering and prototyping studio. Work alongside people who care about an idea, rather than treating them merely as clients handing over requirements. Explore the problem together, prototype something tangible, and test/refine it with the people it is meant to help.

Examples of fit: tools that support real-world exploration, family safety, community participation, intentional living, resource sharing and caring for the natural world. Software and connected devices are possible mediums, not the mission itself.

The founder prefers building with people who care about a problem, including those who cannot afford a conventional agency. Shared ownership (including equity where appropriate), paid engagements and other mutually agreed arrangements can be considered. Clarify contributions, responsibilities, costs and ownership before beginning; do not promise free work, investment, acceptance or particular terms.

## Where we're going
The long-term aspiration is a multidisciplinary, human-centered innovation lab and gathering place organized around a common hearth. Envision a circular hub with distinct spaces where people from different disciplines collaborate:

| Working concept | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Central hearth | Shared ideas, community, connection and collaboration |
| Tinker Town | Engineering, making, prototyping and technology |
| Druid's Grove | Nature, botany and earth sciences |
| Serenity Peak | Reflection, mindfulness and mental well-being |
| Proving Grounds | Movement, physical exploration and growth |
| Science Lab | Experimentation and discovery |
| Art and craft space | Creative expression, design and storytelling |
| Tower of Knowledge | Reading, learning, study and knowledge exchange |

These are working concepts for the future, not facilities that currently exist. The initial engineering studio is the first practical step toward that vision. Make this distinction unmistakable in public copy.

## Decision lens
Does the work improve a real human or ecological outcome? Does it give people more agency, not more dependence? Can we learn something useful by building a small first version? Are the people affected included in the process? Are we being transparent about what exists and what remains aspirational?

Avoid novelty for novelty's sake, extractive engagement, generic agency positioning and technology that distances people from the life they want to live. Mythfits should feel imaginative and grounded at once.
