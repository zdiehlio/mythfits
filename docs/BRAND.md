# Brand | Mythfits

## Identity
Mythfits (wordmark may use MYTHFITS). Core phrase: **Every idea starts as an ember.** Supporting phrase from the prototype: **Together, we bring it to life.** The phoenix represents the transformation from a fragile idea to a realized creation through shared work.

The detailed hero artwork has a scaffold-like unfinished left wing (viewer left), a finished dark metallic feathered right wing, a molten copper/orange body, and a coal base. Preserve this meaningful asymmetry; it is not merely a generic fantasy bird. The founder liked the richer artwork and the initial interactive design. The exact v3 implementation is a reference for review, not a final production approval.

## Visual direction
Cinematic, warm, grounded, tactile and restrained. Near-black surroundings with copper, charcoal, molten highlights and quiet negative space. The ember is an invitation. Use fantasy/mythic imagery as a metaphor for real human creativity, not as an excuse to turn the site into a fantasy game interface. Avoid generic corporate stock imagery, SaaS dashboard aesthetics, excessive gradients, neon clutter and gratuitous animations.

## Voice
Warm, thoughtful, plainspoken, curious and collaborative. Write for a person with an idea, not an investor pitch deck. Use concrete language, shorter paragraphs and honest descriptions of what we do. Avoid hype, corporate jargon, unsupported claims and forced mythical terminology. Prefer natural punctuation over double-dash sentence breaks.

Useful language: "Bring your ember", "Give the idea somewhere to grow", "Explore", "Prototype", "Test & refine", "We build with you". Existing live copy remains the baseline; prototype copy is a creative reference.

## Assets
The local onboarding package contains the source-quality `assets/phoenix/phoenix-master.png`. The site serves the optimized v3 artwork from `mythfits-assets/hero/phoenix-awakening.webp`. Retain transparency; no rectangular matte or visible boundary on the charcoal background. Preserve existing logo assets unless separately approved. Confirm asset rights/provenance and any attribution requirements before public release. Don't generate replacements or recolor approved assets without a reason.
